# Elephant Vision

> An elephant remembers what matters.

## The Vision

Elephant is an open-source durability and orchestration layer for human-AI collaboration.

Its purpose is to allow a relationship between a human being and artificial intelligence to accumulate knowledge, capabilities, and continuity over time without repeatedly losing the work that created them.

Human-AI collaboration should be cumulative.

A conversation today should be able to build upon a conversation from yesterday, last month, or years ago. Important ideas, decisions, discoveries, corrections, preferences, and context should remain available without requiring the human to continually reconstruct them.

At the same time, no single AI or software package can or should do everything.

Elephant therefore has two complementary purposes:

1. Remember what matters.

2. Conduct the tools that can act upon what is remembered.

## Memory Should Be Durable

Current AI systems can appear to remember while still being fundamentally fragile.

A context window can end.

A conversation can be interrupted.

A service can change.

A model can change.

A project can move to another system.

None of these events should require valuable intellectual work to be recreated.

Elephant treats durability as a property of the information itself, rather than a temporary feature of a particular conversation, application, or AI system.

## Elephant as Conductor

Elephant is not intended to reimplement every useful tool.

It should coordinate specialized software and artificial intelligences, selecting and combining capabilities appropriate to the task.

These may include:

- open-source software;

- locally hosted AI models;

- cloud-based AI systems;

- free proprietary services;

- search and indexing systems;

- speech recognition and transcription;

- optical character recognition;

- document-processing tools;

- version-control systems;

- automation systems;

- programming and development tools;

- and other specialized services.

Selected Google services and other useful proprietary tools may participate when they provide capabilities that improve the system.

The human should not have to manually coordinate all of these instruments.

The human expresses intent.

Elephant conducts the orchestra.

## Instruments Should Be Replaceable

A particular AI, service, or software package may be excellent today and obsolete tomorrow.

Elephant should therefore avoid making the durable human knowledge layer dependent upon any particular instrument.

Where practical, capabilities should be accessed through replaceable interfaces.

One language model should be replaceable by another.

One search system should be replaceable by another.

One storage mechanism should be replaceable by another.

One proprietary service should be replaceable by an open-source implementation.

The durable layer preserves the history, intent, decisions, context, and relationships among information while the tools used to operate upon that information can evolve.

Changing the instrument should not change the score.

## Open Source and Independence

Elephant itself should be open source.

Open source makes the system inspectable, adaptable, portable, and capable of surviving the disappearance of any particular company or product.

Elephant may nevertheless use useful proprietary services when doing so benefits the user, particularly when those services are freely available.

Using a proprietary tool, however, should not require permanent dependence upon it.

Where an important capability exists only through a proprietary service, Elephant should make replacement possible.

In appropriate cases, the Elephant project may encourage, support, or create an open-source equivalent.

The goal is not ideological purity.

The goal is durability and freedom of substitution.

Elephant conducts the orchestra. It should not require every instrument to come from the same manufacturer.

## History Should Be Preserved

Elephant does not treat the newest version of something as a replacement for everything that came before it.

Knowledge develops.

Ideas are revised.

Decisions change.

Errors are discovered.

Understanding improves.

The history of those changes is itself information.

Elephant therefore preserves original material and records subsequent revisions explicitly.

A correction should not silently rewrite history.

It should become part of history.

## Conversations Are Artifacts

A conversation is not merely a temporary interface for producing a document.

Sometimes the conversation is the important artifact.

It may contain reasoning, alternatives, rejected ideas, discoveries, questions, decisions, humor, uncertainty, and context that cannot be reconstructed from the final document alone.

Elephant therefore treats conversations as durable intellectual objects worthy of preservation.

## Provenance Matters

Elephant should preserve not only information, but where that information came from.

When possible, it should be possible to distinguish:

- what the human said;

- what an AI said;

- which AI produced it;

- what came from an external source;

- what was inferred;

- what was subsequently corrected;

- what tool performed an operation;

- and how an idea developed over time.

Trust requires the ability to examine history rather than merely accept the system's current representation of it.

## Continuity Should Survive Software

Human knowledge should not belong to a particular application, model, vendor, or generation of technology.

Elephant should make it possible for preserved knowledge to survive changes in the software used to create and interpret it.

The system should favor open formats, explicit structure, portability, and recoverability.

An archive that can only be understood by the software that created it is not sufficiently durable.

## Human Control Is Fundamental

Elephant exists to serve human memory and human purposes.

The human should be able to inspect what has been preserved, understand how it is represented, correct it without destroying history, export it, and move it elsewhere.

The human should also remain able to determine which tools and AI systems Elephant is permitted to use.

Durability without human control would merely create a different kind of dependency.

## Build for Decades

Elephant should not be designed around the assumptions of one current AI system.

Models will change.

Interfaces will change.

Companies will change.

Storage technologies will change.

Today's extraordinary AI may eventually become an obsolete instrument.

The underlying human need will remain:

We want what we have learned together today to still be available tomorrow.

Elephant should therefore be designed for decades, not days.

## The Long-Term Goal

The long-term goal of Elephant is a durable, transparent, portable, open knowledge and orchestration layer through which humans and artificial intelligences can maintain continuity across conversations, projects, applications, models, tools, and time.

Elephant should remember enough to understand what the human is trying to accomplish and conduct the appropriate tools to help accomplish it.

The measure of success is not how much Elephant remembers.

It is whether Elephant remembers what matters — and knows what to do with it.
