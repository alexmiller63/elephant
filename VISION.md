# Elephant Vision

> An elephant remembers what matters.

## The Vision

Elephant exists to make long-term human-AI collaboration durable.

Human beings and artificial intelligence can develop ideas together over hours, days, months, or years. Those ideas should not depend on a single conversation, a particular context window, one application, or one generation of software.

Elephant is intended to provide continuity across those boundaries.

The goal is not simply to remember information. The goal is to preserve the development of knowledge.

A decision should retain the reasoning that produced it.

A revision should not erase the thing it revised.

A document should remain connected to the conversations and ideas from which it developed.

A project should be able to accumulate history without requiring the human or the AI to repeatedly reconstruct that history.

## Durable Human-AI Collaboration

Elephant treats continuity as infrastructure.

It should allow a human and an AI to stop working and later resume with the important context intact.

It should allow one AI system to understand work previously performed with another.

It should allow software to change without making the user's accumulated knowledge obsolete.

The human should remain able to inspect what has been preserved, where it came from, how it changed, and why.

## History Without Erasure

Elephant does not treat the newest version as the only version that matters.

Original material remains original.

Corrections remain corrections.

Revisions remain revisions.

Later interpretations do not silently replace earlier ones.

The history of an idea is itself information worth preserving.

## Knowledge That Grows

Most software stores information.

Elephant is intended to preserve continuity.

Its purpose is to allow conversations, documents, decisions, observations, and ideas to accumulate into a durable body of knowledge that can continue developing over time.

Instead of repeatedly rebuilding context, humans and AI should be able to build upon what they have already learned together.

## Long-Term Goal

Elephant should ultimately be independent of any single AI model, application, company, device, or storage system.

Its architecture should favor open formats, transparent history, portability, and recoverability.

The project should be designed with a time horizon measured in decades rather than product cycles.

Elephant succeeds when important human-AI work can survive interruption, migration, revision, and technological change without losing its history or meaning.
